OVA Privacy Policy

Understand your cycle. Understand yourself.

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OVA — Period Tracker and Wellness (“OVA,” “we,” or “our”) handles information when you use the OVA mobile application.

Information you enter

OVA is designed to let you record cycle and wellness information such as period dates, symptoms, mood, sleep, activity, water intake, and personal notes. This information may be sensitive. In the app version described by this policy, entries are saved locally on your device using on-device storage. We do not operate an OVA account system for these entries, and OVA does not send these entries to an OVA-operated server.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, OVA may schedule notifications on your device. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings. Notification behavior depends on the app settings and permissions you choose.

Information collected by services and SDKs

The app may rely on platform services or third-party libraries to function. Those providers may process technical information under their own privacy policies. Before relying on this policy for a release, the app publisher should verify all included SDKs and their behavior, including crash reporting, analytics, advertising, and update services, and update this section if any such service collects or shares data.

How to delete your information

Information stored only on your device can generally be removed by using any data-deletion controls provided in OVA, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove locally saved entries. If the app provides an in-app “Delete all data” option, use that option to remove your saved entries.

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact us at ova.contact.team@gmail.com. Because locally stored information is not held on our servers, we cannot remotely delete data that remains only on your device.

Children's privacy

OVA is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an OVA-operated account system. If you believe information has been provided to us directly, contact us at the email above.

Security and retention

Locally stored entries remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup and operating-system settings. Keep your device secured and use available device-lock features to help protect sensitive information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published at this page with an updated effective date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact ova.contact.team@gmail.com.
